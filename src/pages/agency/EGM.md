# Agency form pages (`src/pages/agency`)

## Overview

This directory has no page file of its own. It groups the two public agency suggestion forms. Agency profile pages are not served from here.

## Subdirectories & Boundaries

- new: [./new/EGM.md](./new/EGM.md) - Form page `/agency/new/` that submits the `agencyNew` form to suggest a new agency profile.
- suggest-edit: [./suggest-edit/EGM.md](./suggest-edit/EGM.md) - Form page `/agency/suggest-edit/` that submits the `agencyEdit` form to suggest a correction to an agency profile.
