What the complaint says

Steven Nix, Jonathan Sorrelli, and Matthew Alarcon bring a putative § 1983 class action against the City of Irving alleging Fourth and Fourteenth Amendment due-process violations from a written May 1, 2024 prisoner arraignment and transport policy.

The complaint alleges Irving denied Class B-or-above arrestees held on Dallas County charges access to Irving Municipal Judges or Dallas County magistrates while they remained in Irving Jail, transferred them to Dallas County instead, and caused named plaintiffs and similarly situated people to wait 12 or more hours — but less than 48 hours — to see a judge.

Exhibit A to the complaint is an April 30, 2024 memo from Deputy Chief Michael Braly to Jail Manager Shari Harding titled "Changes to Prisoner Arraignment and Prisoner Transport Process."
