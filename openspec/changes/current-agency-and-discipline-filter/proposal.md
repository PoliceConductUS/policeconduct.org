## Why

Former-only agency assignments still qualify empty-looking agencies for pages. Visitors also need to find agency personnel with recorded discipline without scanning the entire roster.

## What Changes

- Require at least one current personnel assignment for agency office projections, federal office lists/counts and agency redirects.
- Preserve every root federal agency page and directory entry.
- Add a discipline-only checkbox to agency personnel rosters, combining with status and name filters.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `agency-browsing`: Current agency eligibility and roster discipline filtering.

## Impact

Projection and redirect eligibility, federal office loaders, agency roster component, tests and current specifications. No database schema changes, intake mutations, new dependencies or deployment during the import.
